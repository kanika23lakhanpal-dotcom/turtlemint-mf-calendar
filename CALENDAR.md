## LEVEL 0: MONEY & INVESTING BASICS
*From Zero to Financial Awareness: Why Save? Why Invest?*

### Module M00.1: The Nature of Money, Savings & Inflation

#### Lesson L00.01: What is Money & Purchasing Power?
- **Core Topic:** The Real Meaning of Money
- **Format & Duration:** Interactive Explainer + Animated Short (8 mins)
- **Granular Subtopics:**
  * Barter system to paper currency: How money represents stored human labor
  * Nominal wealth vs. Real purchasing power: The ₹100 note experiment over 20 years
  * Why money left idle in lockers or almirahs loses value every single day
  * Delayed consumption: Sacrificing today's spending for tomorrow's security
  * The three functions of money: Medium of exchange, unit of account, store of value
- **What DP Must KNOW:** Money is only as valuable as what it can buy in the market at a given point in time.
- **What DP Must UNDERSTAND:** Holding physical cash creates an illusion of safety; your ₹500 note stays ₹500, but buys fewer goods each year.
- **What DP Must DO (Turtlemint Pro/Ninja Action):** Show the customer historical price trends of everyday staples (milk, petrol, school fees) over 10-20 years.
- **What DP Must SAY (Hinglish Script):** "Sharma ji, locker mein rakha note chori nahi hota, par uski taakat har saal kam hoti jaati hai."
- **What DP Must NEVER CLAIM (Compliance Red Line):** Never claim that physical cash or savings accounts are completely useless—they serve immediate liquidity needs.

----------------------------------------

#### Lesson L00.02: Saving vs. Investing: The Critical Divide
- **Core Topic:** Storage vs. Wealth Creation
- **Format & Duration:** Visual Comparison Grid + Whiteboard Video (10 mins)
- **Granular Subtopics:**
  * What is Saving? Parking money safely for immediate emergency use
  * What is Investing? Putting money to work in productive economic assets
  * The Warehouse vs. Seed Analogy: Storing grain vs. planting seeds in fertile soil
  * Why savings accounts can never fund 15-year future goals like children's higher education
  * The dual-bucket household approach: Emergency buffer (Savings) + Growth engine (Investing)
- **What DP Must KNOW:** Saving protects principal in the ultra short term; investing takes calculated risk to beat inflation over time.
- **What DP Must UNDERSTAND:** A family that only saves will end up with capital depletion in real terms over decades.
- **What DP Must DO (Turtlemint Pro/Ninja Action):** Calculate total returns of ₹5,000/month saved in a 3.5% savings account vs a productive asset over 15 years.
- **What DP Must SAY (Hinglish Script):** "Saving emergency ke liye hoti hai, aur investing sapne poore karne ke liye."
- **What DP Must NEVER CLAIM (Compliance Red Line):** Never tell a customer to invest 100% of their money without keeping an emergency savings fund.

----------------------------------------

#### Lesson L00.03: The Silent Thief: Inflation & The Rule of 72
- **Core Topic:** Purchasing Power Erosion & Mental Math
- **Format & Duration:** Interactive Slider Tool + Micro-Podcast (12 mins)
- **Granular Subtopics:**
  * What is Inflation (Mehangai)? Consumer Price Index (CPI) vs Lifestyle Inflation
  * Why education and healthcare inflate at 8-10% annually while headline CPI is 5-6%
  * The Real Return Formula: Real Return = Nominal Return - Inflation Rate - Taxes
  * The Rule of 72: How many years it takes for prices to double (72 ÷ Inflation Rate)
  * The Ice Cube Analogy: Why cash in a bank account melts over time
- **What DP Must KNOW:** If inflation is 6% and return is 6%, real growth is 0%. If taxes apply, real return is negative.
- **What DP Must UNDERSTAND:** Beating inflation is not a luxury or greed; it is fundamental family financial self-defense.
- **What DP Must DO (Turtlemint Pro/Ninja Action):** Use Turtlemint Pro's Inflation Calculator to project a child's college fees 12 years into the future.
- **What DP Must SAY (Hinglish Script):** "Agar aapka return mehangai se kam hai, toh aap dheere-dheere gareeb ho rahe hain."
- **What DP Must NEVER CLAIM (Compliance Red Line):** Never promise that mutual funds eliminate inflation entirely; they seek to generate an inflation-beating spread.

### Module M00.2: The Asset Class Landscape & Risk-Return Basics

#### Lesson L00.04: The Indian Asset Landscape: FD, Gold, Real Estate & Stocks
- **Core Topic:** Comparing Traditional vs Modern Assets
- **Format & Duration:** Comparison Matrix + Infographic Poster (14 mins)
- **Granular Subtopics:**

  * Where do Mutual Funds fit? An accessible bridge to institutional gold, debt, and equity
- **What DP Must KNOW:** Each asset class has unique liquidity, ticket size, volatility, tax, and inflation-beating properties.
- **What DP Must UNDERSTAND:** No single asset class is superior for every goal; each fulfills a distinct family financial purpose.
- **What DP Must DO (Turtlemint Pro/Ninja Action):** Map a customer's existing assets into a 4-quadrant liquidity and growth worksheet.
- **What DP Must SAY (Hinglish Script):** "Hum traditional assets ki respect karte hain, lekin har asset ka apna sahi role hota hai."
- **What DP Must NEVER CLAIM (Compliance Red Line):** Never criticize bank FDs or Gold as 'bad' investments; acknowledge their stability while highlighting limitations.

#### Lesson L00.05: The Universal Law: Risk vs. Return
- **Core Topic:** The Price of Wealth Creation
- **Format & Duration:** Animated Case Study + Quiz (10 mins)
- **Granular Subtopics:**

- **What DP Must UNDERSTAND:** Any scheme promising high returns without market fluctuations is either an unviable promise or a financial fraud.

## LEVEL 1: INTRODUCTION TO MUTUAL FUNDS
*How Pooling Money Democratizes Professional Wealth Management*

### Module M01.1: The Pooling Mechanism & Legal Structure

#### Lesson L01.01: What is a Mutual Fund? The Power of Collective Capital
- **Core Topic:** The Core Concept of Pooling
- **Format & Duration:** Character-Driven Narrative Animation (10 mins)
- **Granular Subtopics:**
  * The Tractor Analogy: 100 small farmers pooling ₹10,000 each to buy a ₹10 Lakh commercial tractor
  * Why an individual cannot buy 50 top Indian company shares with ₹1,000 alone
  * How collective pooling unlocks institutional economies of scale and professional management
  * The Mutual Fund as an Investment Trust, NOT a manufacturing corporate company
  * Pass-through vehicle: Profits and losses belong directly to the unit-holders in exact proportion
- **What DP Must KNOW:** A mutual fund is an institutional vehicle that pools capital from millions of investors to purchase securities.
- **What DP Must UNDERSTAND:** The fund company does not 'take' your money; they merely manage a ring-fenced trust on your behalf.
- **What DP Must DO (Turtlemint Pro/Ninja Action):** Walk a new DP through how their own ₹500 SIP gets combined with a ₹5,000 Crore scheme portfolio.
- **What DP Must SAY (Hinglish Script):** "Mutual fund ka matlab hai: Chota paisa milkar ban gaya bada fund, jise top experts manage karte hain."
- **What DP Must NEVER CLAIM (Compliance Red Line):** Never say 'Mutual fund ek company hai jo hume munafa deti hai'—it is a trust passing through market returns.

----------------------------------------

#### Lesson L01.02: Anatomy of a Unit & The Net Asset Value (NAV)
- **Core Topic:** Understanding NAV Without Jargon
- **Format & Duration:** Interactive Pizza-Slicing Simulator (12 mins)
- **Granular Subtopics:**
  * What is a Unit? The measure of your proportional ownership in the scheme
  * What is NAV? Net Asset Value = (Total Assets - Liabilities) ÷ Total Outstanding Units
  * Daily calculation: Why NAV updates every evening based on closing market prices
  * The Pizza Slice Analogy: 4 large slices vs 8 small slices—the quantity of pizza stays identical
  * Dismantling the ₹10 NAV vs ₹100 NAV myth: Why a low NAV does NOT mean a fund is 'cheap'
- **What DP Must KNOW:** NAV is simply the per-unit mathematical value derived from current market prices of underlying holdings.
- **What DP Must UNDERSTAND:** A fund with NAV of ₹20 is not 10 times 'cheaper' than a fund with NAV of ₹200; percentage growth is what matters.
- **What DP Must DO (Turtlemint Pro/Ninja Action):** Run a live calculation in Turtlemint Ninja showing ₹10,000 invested in a ₹10 NAV fund vs ₹100 NAV fund.
- **What DP Must SAY (Hinglish Script):** "NAV share price ki tarah sasta ya mehenga nahi hota, yeh sirf aapke hisse ka hisaab-kitaab hai."
- **What DP Must NEVER CLAIM (Compliance Red Line):** Never tell an investor that an NFO is 'cheap' because its starting NAV is ₹10.

----------------------------------------

#### Lesson L01.03: AUM, Inflows, Outflows & Fractional Units
- **Core Topic:** How Scheme Scale Operates
- **Format & Duration:** Interactive Flowchart + Knowledge Check (10 mins)
- **Granular Subtopics:**
  * What is Assets Under Management (AUM)? Total market value of all investor capital in the fund
  * How fresh investments create new units and redemptions extinguish existing units
  * Fractional unit allotment: How mutual funds track investments down to 4 decimal places (e.g., 23.4567 units)
  * What happens when ₹1,000 is invested: Step-by-step allotment journey from bank to folio
  * Open-ended vs Closed-ended schemes: Unlimited liquidity vs fixed lock-in tenures
- **What DP Must KNOW:** AUM expands with new inflows and market growth, and contracts with redemptions and market drops.
- **What DP Must UNDERSTAND:** Open-ended mutual funds provide continuous entry and exit at prevailing NAV without liquidity traps.
- **What DP Must DO (Turtlemint Pro/Ninja Action):** Show a real Turtlemint statement highlighting units held to 4 decimal places.
- **What DP Must SAY (Hinglish Script):** "Aapka ek-ek paisa unit ke roop mein 4 decimal tak accurately account hota hai."
- **What DP Must NEVER CLAIM (Compliance Red Line):** Never claim that a higher AUM automatically makes a fund the best performing in its category.

### Module M01.2: How Investors Earn & Why Returns Fluctuate

#### Lesson L01.04: Two Engines of Return: Capital Growth vs IDCW
- **Core Topic:** Capital Appreciation vs Cash Distribution
- **Format & Duration:** Real Estate Rental Analogy Video (11 mins)
- **Granular Subtopics:**
  * Capital Appreciation (Growth Option): Profits remain reinvested in the fund, driving NAV upward
  * Income Distribution cum Capital Withdrawal (IDCW): Periodic payouts from accumulated reserves
  * The House Analogy: Appreciation in property price (Growth) vs receiving monthly rent (IDCW)
  * Crucial realization: IDCW is NOT a bonus; on payout day, the scheme NAV falls by the exact payout amount
  * Why the Growth option is overwhelmingly superior for wealth compounding and tax efficiency
- **What DP Must KNOW:** IDCW reduces the NAV by the dividend amount; it is not free or additional money.
- **What DP Must UNDERSTAND:** Growth option unleashes uninterrupted compounding because profits are never drained out to pay taxes prematurely.
- **What DP Must DO (Turtlemint Pro/Ninja Action):** Show a comparison chart of Growth NAV vs IDCW NAV of the same scheme over 10 years.
- **What DP Must SAY (Hinglish Script):** "Growth option mein aapka munafa wapas business mein lagta hai taaki compounding ka poora fayda mile."
- **What DP Must NEVER CLAIM (Compliance Red Line):** Never pitch IDCW as a 'guaranteed monthly salary' or a 'bonus from the fund company'.

----------------------------------------

#### Lesson L01.05: Why Mutual Funds are Market-Linked & Never Guaranteed
- **Core Topic:** The Foundation of Transparency
- **Format & Duration:** Compliance Animated Explainer (9 mins)
- **Granular Subtopics:**
  * Mark-to-Market (MTM) accounting: Portfolios are valued every single day at active exchange prices
  * Why mutual funds can have negative returns over short time horizons (1 day, 1 month, 1 year)
  * The difference between temporary market volatility and permanent business loss
  * Why SEBI explicitly outlaws guaranteed returns in mutual funds
  * Professional Fund Management: Research analysts, company visits, quantitative risk models
- **What DP Must KNOW:** All mutual funds are legally pass-through market-linked instruments without sovereign or AMC guarantees.
- **What DP Must UNDERSTAND:** Market linkage is the exact reason why mutual funds can deliver superior inflation-beating returns over time.
- **What DP Must DO (Turtlemint Pro/Ninja Action):** Walk a customer through a 5-year NAV chart showing sharp drawdowns followed by steady new highs.
- **What DP Must SAY (Hinglish Script):** "Mutual fund market-linked hote hain, isiliye short-term mein upar-neeche honge, par long-term mein growth denge."
- **What DP Must NEVER CLAIM (Compliance Red Line):** NEVER use words like 'guaranteed', 'fixed', 'risk-free', or 'sure-shot' in any mutual fund conversation.

## LEVEL 2: THE MUTUAL FUND ECOSYSTEM
*Who Protects the Investor's Capital? Trust, Custody & Governance*

### Module M02.1: The Three-Tier Trust Structure & Regulators

#### Lesson L02.01: The Legal Ring-Fence: Sponsor, Trustee & AMC
- **Core Topic:** How Investor Wealth is Legally Protected
- **Format & Duration:** Architectural Blueprint Explainer (12 mins)
- **Granular Subtopics:**
  * Why the AMC does NOT own your money: The Indian Trust Act & SEBI MF Regulations 1996
  * The Sponsor: The promoter who sets up the mutual fund trust (like a parent company)
  * The Board of Trustees: Independent fiduciaries whose sole legal duty is safeguarding unit-holders
  * The Asset Management Company (AMC): The operational agency hired by trustees to manage portfolios
  * The Ultimate Safety Question: What happens if the AMC shuts down or goes bankrupt? (Assets remain 100% safe)
- **What DP Must KNOW:** The scheme's assets are held in a separate legal trust, completely isolated from AMC liabilities.
- **What DP Must UNDERSTAND:** Even if an AMC goes bankrupt, creditors cannot touch a single rupee of investor mutual fund holdings.
- **What DP Must DO (Turtlemint Pro/Ninja Action):** Show a client where the names of the Trustees are listed on the front page of a Scheme Information Document (SID).
- **What DP Must SAY (Hinglish Script):** "AMC sirf caretaker hai, maalik aap hain aur pehredaar Trustee aur SEBI hain."
- **What DP Must NEVER CLAIM (Compliance Red Line):** Never claim that SEBI guarantees fund performance; SEBI regulates process, disclosures, and integrity.

----------------------------------------

#### Lesson L02.02: SEBI & AMFI: The Regulators and Watchdogs
- **Core Topic:** Regulatory Framework & Investor Rights
- **Format & Duration:** Compliance Infographic + Interactive Case File (10 mins)
- **Granular Subtopics:**
  * SEBI (Securities and Exchange Board of India): Statutory regulator with punitive legal powers
  * SEBI's mandate: Investor protection, fair market conduct, continuous disclosure standards
  * AMFI (Association of Mutual Funds in India): Industry body, code of conduct, ARN issuance, investor awareness

- **What DP Must KNOW:** SEBI sets iron-clad guidelines on what AMCs can buy, how they disclose, and how distributors operate.
- **What DP Must UNDERSTAND:** The Indian mutual fund ecosystem is among the most transparent and tightly regulated financial systems globally.
- **What DP Must DO (Turtlemint Pro/Ninja Action):** Demonstrate the SEBI SCORES portal workflow on a smartphone screen.
- **What DP Must SAY (Hinglish Script):** "Aapka har transaction SEBI ke strict rules aur regular audits ke under hota hai."
- **What DP Must NEVER CLAIM (Compliance Red Line):** Never tell a customer they have no recourse in case of operational issues; point to SCORES and ODR.

## LEVEL 3: TYPES OF MUTUAL FUNDS
*Mastering the SEBI Categorization: Equity, Debt, Hybrid & Passives*

### Module M03.1: Equity Fund Categories (SEBI Framework)

#### Lesson L03.01: Market Cap Classification: Large, Mid & Small Cap
- **Core Topic:** Understanding Size, Stability & Volatility
- **Format & Duration:** Banyan Tree vs Sapling Visualizer (14 mins)
- **Granular Subtopics:**
  * SEBI Market Cap Definitions: Top 100 (Large Cap), 101-250 (Mid Cap), 251st onwards (Small Cap)
  * Large Cap Funds: Investing min 80% in top 100 giants (Elephants: Stable, resilient, lower volatility)
  * Mid Cap Funds: Min 65% in mid-sized businesses (Horses: Growth potential with higher volatility)
  * Small Cap Funds: Min 65% in small emerging enterprises (Rabbits: High growth potential, severe drawdowns)
  * Horizon matching: Why Small Caps require 7-10+ years and Large Caps suit 5+ years
- **What DP Must KNOW:** Large caps provide portfolio stability; small caps offer high growth potential but experience sharp 30-50% drawdowns.
- **What DP Must UNDERSTAND:** Putting a conservative first-time investor into a pure Small Cap fund is a recipe for panic selling during corrections.
- **What DP Must DO (Turtlemint Pro/Ninja Action):** Check the market cap breakdown pie chart of any equity scheme on Turtlemint Pro.
- **What DP Must SAY (Hinglish Script):** "Large cap banyan tree jaisa majboot hai, small cap chote paudhe jaisa jo tezi se badh sakta hai par toofan mein hilta hai."
- **What DP Must NEVER CLAIM (Compliance Red Line):** Never claim that Small Cap funds will always deliver higher returns than Large Cap funds over any period.

----------------------------------------

#### Lesson L03.02: Diversified Equity: Flexi Cap, Multi Cap & Focused Funds
- **Core Topic:** Manager Freedom vs Mandated Allocation
- **Format & Duration:** Strategy Comparison Table + Case Study (13 mins)
- **Granular Subtopics:**
  * Multi Cap Funds: SEBI 25-25-25 rule (Min 25% Large, 25% Mid, 25% Small Cap at all times)
  * Flexi Cap Funds: 100% dynamic freedom for fund managers across market caps (Min 65% equity)
  * Focused Funds: Maximum 30 stocks—high conviction, high concentration risk
  * Large & Mid Cap Funds: Min 35% in Large Cap and min 35% in Mid Cap
  * Which suits a first-time investor? Why Flexi Cap is often the core starting building block
- **What DP Must KNOW:** Flexi Cap gives fund managers full tactical flexibility, while Multi Cap enforces structural discipline.
- **What DP Must UNDERSTAND:** Focused funds carry high stock-specific risk; if 3 out of 30 stocks face corporate governance issues, NAV suffers.
- **What DP Must DO (Turtlemint Pro/Ninja Action):** Compare stock count and top 10 holding percentages between a Flexi Cap and Focused fund.
- **What DP Must SAY (Hinglish Script):** "Flexi cap fund manager ko azaadi deta hai ki jahan opportunity ho, wahan paisa lagaye."
- **What DP Must NEVER CLAIM (Compliance Red Line):** Never tell an investor that a Focused fund carries the same risk as a broad-market Flexi Cap fund.

----------------------------------------

#### Lesson L03.03: Style-Based Equity: Value, Contra, Dividend Yield & ELSS
- **Core Topic:** Investment Styles & Tax Benefits
- **Format & Duration:** Interactive Matrix + Tax Calculator (12 mins)
- **Granular Subtopics:**
  * Value Investing: Buying undervalued, unloved quality businesses trading at a discount (P/E, P/B)
  * Contra Funds: Betting against current market consensus and out-of-favor sectors
  * Dividend Yield Funds: Investing min 65% in high dividend-paying cash-rich companies
  * ELSS (Equity Linked Savings Scheme): Min 80% equity, 3-year statutory lock-in, Section 80C eligibility
  * Old Tax Regime vs New Tax Regime: When does ELSS still make sense for an investor?
- **What DP Must KNOW:** ELSS has the shortest lock-in (3 years) among all Section 80C tax-saving financial instruments.
- **What DP Must UNDERSTAND:** Value/Contra strategies require immense patience; they can underperform growth rallies for 2-3 years before surging.
- **What DP Must DO (Turtlemint Pro/Ninja Action):** Check whether a customer is filing taxes under Old or New Regime before discussing ELSS schemes.
- **What DP Must SAY (Hinglish Script):** "ELSS 3 saal ke lock-in ke sath disciplined equity investing sikhata hai."
- **What DP Must NEVER CLAIM (Compliance Red Line):** Never promise tax deductions on ELSS if the client is opted into the New Tax Regime where 80C is unavailable.

----------------------------------------

#### Lesson L03.04: High-Risk Bets: Sectoral & Thematic Funds
- **Core Topic:** Concentration & Timing Risks
- **Format & Duration:** Boom-and-Bust Overlay Chart Video (11 mins)
- **Granular Subtopics:**
  * Sectoral Funds: Min 80% in one single industry (Banking, Pharma, Technology, Auto, Infra)
  * Thematic Funds: Min 80% in an economic theme spanning multiple sectors (Manufacturing, Consumption, ESG)
  * The Cyclical Trap: Why sectors boom for 2 years and remain stagnant for the next 4 years
  * The Entry/Exit Timing Problem: Why ordinary retail investors almost always enter at the top
  * The Golden Portfolio Rule: Sectoral funds should rarely exceed 10-15% of a client's portfolio
- **What DP Must KNOW:** Sectoral funds lack cross-industry diversification; if that specific sector enters a recession, the entire fund drops.
- **What DP Must UNDERSTAND:** Past 1-year top returns on sector funds are usually a warning sign of peak euphoria, not a buy signal.
- **What DP Must DO (Turtlemint Pro/Ninja Action):** Show historical 5-year cyclical charts of Pharma (2015-2020) and IT (2021-2023) on Turtlemint Ninja.
- **What DP Must SAY (Hinglish Script):** "Sector fund mein ek hi tokri mein saare ande hote hain; jab tak experience na ho, diversified funds hi best hain."
- **What DP Must NEVER CLAIM (Compliance Red Line):** Never recommend a pure sectoral fund to a first-time or conservative investor.

### Module M03.2: Debt Funds, Hybrid Funds & Passives

#### Lesson L03.05: Debt Mutual Funds: What are You Actually Buying?
- **Core Topic:** Lending to Governments & Corporates
- **Format & Duration:** Interactive Debt Instrument Explorer (14 mins)

  * Corporate Bond Funds (Min 80% in AA+ and above) vs Credit Risk Funds (Min 65% in below AA)

- **What DP Must UNDERSTAND:** Credit risk funds seek higher yields by lending to weaker companies; defaults can permanently wipe out capital.
- **What DP Must DO (Turtlemint Pro/Ninja Action):** Inspect the credit rating breakdown (AAA, SOV, AA, A1+) of a debt fund on Turtlemint Pro.

#### Lesson L03.06: Hybrid Funds: The Automatic Shock Absorbers
- **Core Topic:** Asset Allocation in a Single Scheme
- **Format & Duration:** Animated Shock-Absorber Mechanism (14 mins)
- **Granular Subtopics:**
  * Aggressive Hybrid Funds: 65-80% Equity + 20-35% Debt (Classic wealth creation with a cushion)
  * Conservative Hybrid Funds: 10-25% Equity + 75-90% Debt (Income with a touch of inflation defense)
  * Balanced Advantage / Dynamic Asset Allocation (BAF): Dynamic 0-100% equity based on valuation models
  * Multi-Asset Allocation Funds: Min 10% in at least 3 distinct asset classes (Equity, Debt, Gold/Silver)
  * Arbitrage Funds: Exploiting cash-futures market spreads for safe, equity-taxed short-term returns
- **What DP Must KNOW:** Balanced Advantage Funds dynamically reduce equity when markets are expensive and increase equity when cheap.
- **What DP Must UNDERSTAND:** Hybrid funds protect investors from their own greed and fear by automating rebalancing behind the scenes.
- **What DP Must DO (Turtlemint Pro/Ninja Action):** Show how an Aggressive Hybrid fund had much lower drawdowns than a pure equity fund during March 2020.
- **What DP Must SAY (Hinglish Script):** "Hybrid fund car ke shock absorber jaisa hai—khaddom mein jhatka kam lagne deta hai."
- **What DP Must NEVER CLAIM (Compliance Red Line):** Never state that Balanced Advantage Funds can never experience negative returns in short time windows.

#### Lesson L03.07: Passive Funds: Index Funds, ETFs & Fund of Funds (FoF)
- **Core Topic:** Low-Cost Index Tracking
